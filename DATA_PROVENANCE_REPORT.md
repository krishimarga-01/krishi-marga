## 3. Data Integrity & Anti-Hallucination Safeguards

1. **Zero-Inference Rule for Agrochemicals**:
   - No active ingredient dosage is ever estimated, extrapolated, or guessed by machine learning models.
   - If a CIBRC label claim does not explicitly cover a specific crop-pest pairing, KRISHI MARGA displays:  
     `"No approved CIBRC chemical label claim found for this combination. Consult your local Agricultural Extension Officer or KVK specialist."`
2. **Double-Blind University Contact Verification**:
   - Every contact in [`SOUTH_INDIA_CROP_DOCTOR_DIRECTORY.csv`](file:///C:/Users/Prathach%20Raj%20P/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/krishi-marga/agricultural_knowledge/SOUTH_INDIA_CROP_DOCTOR_DIRECTORY.csv) was verified against published university staff rosters, ICAR institutional directories, and official government telecommunication rosters.
   - Where a district lacked a dedicated, publicly accessible plant pathology diagnostic lab, it was explicitly marked `NO VERIFIED PUBLIC CONTACT FOUND`.
