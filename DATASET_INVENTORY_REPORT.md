## 1. Executive Summary

A systematic inventory of public, peer-reviewed, and institutional agricultural image datasets was conducted across IEEE DataPort, Kaggle, Mendeley Data, Zenodo, Roboflow Universe, PlantVillage, PlantDoc, and ICAR Research Institutes.

Every dataset in this registry has been verified for:
1. **Traceable Origin & Legal License**: CC0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, or MIT Open Licenses.
2. **Ground Truth Labels**: Pathogen or condition names extracted directly from repository annotations without fabrication.
3. **South India Agricultural Alignment**: Matching crops and regional disease strains.

## 3. Critical Datasets to Avoid / Handle with Caution

1. **Unchecked PlantVillage Mirrors on Kaggle**:
   - *Issue*: Hundreds of repackaged mirrors with duplicated test splits, arbitrary resizing, and severe label corruption.
   - *Rule*: Only use verified original Mendeley / Penn State releases; run SHA-256 deduplication.
2. **Web-Scraped Uncurated Image Dumps**:
   - *Issue*: Generic Google Image crawls often mislabel simple sun scorch or nutrient deficiencies as viral infections.
   - *Rule*: Exclude unvalidated web scrapes; require university/ICAR ground truth.
3. **Artificially Augmented Kaggle Sets (Rotated copies saved as raw)**:
   - *Issue*: Causes severe 99% accuracy illusion due to augmented duplicates leaking into validation/test splits.
   - *Rule*: All augmentation must occur purely dynamically inside the PyTorch `DataLoader` during training, never stored statically in raw data folders.
